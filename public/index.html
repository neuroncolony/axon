How it works

Every trade pays two percent. That two percent buys thinking.

Read the docs →
1 · Launch

Name it, pick the model it backs, pay the pons launch fee. The whole supply goes to a bonding curve.

2 · Trade

Every buy and sell pays a 2% creator tax into the platform treasury, in ETH, never in the token.

3 · Sweep

A keeper claims each curve's escrow to the treasury. That step is off-chain and its wallet is public.

4 · Spend

Launch a token and talk to any backed model. Each reply is billed to the pool by the token at list price.

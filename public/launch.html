What happens
  • Launch fee: …, paid to the pons v2 factory. Do not send ETH directly to the factory or treasury. The 0.0005 ETH fee rides on the launch transaction your wallet signs here; a bare transfer is lost.
  • A 2% creator tax on every curve trade goes to the treasury ….
  • The whole supply goes on a bonding curve. Price rises with buys, falls with sells.
  • At graduation the curve's liquidity moves to a Uniswap v4 pool and the token trades there.
Honest limits
  • pons v2 is unaudited. Contracts can lose funds.
  • The keeper sweep that moves escrow to the treasury is off-chain. Its wallet is public.
  • The model a token backs can be changed later only by relaunching a new token.

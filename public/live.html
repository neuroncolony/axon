How fees flow

Every trade on a curve pays a 3% fee in ETH: 1% pons base fee plus the 2% creator tax. Fees accrue on the curve, are swept to the pons escrow, and the compute pool claims them to fund model replies. The treasury wallet claims the creator tax share here.
